A bit about me

Who I Am

I'm Wainise Draunidalo — a final-year Computer and Information Systems student at the University of the South Pacific in Fiji. My passion sits at the intersection of technology and community: I believe every business, no matter how small or how limited their budget, deserves a shot at going digital.

I focus on helping under-developed businesses that struggle to financially kickstart a digital workforce. That means building simple, maintainable, cost-effective solutions — websites, systems, and tools that genuinely move the needle without overwhelming a small team.

Growing up in Fiji, I've seen firsthand how the digital divide holds local businesses back. I want to be part of changing that — one project at a time.
